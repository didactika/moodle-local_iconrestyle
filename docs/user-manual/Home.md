# Icon Restyle user manual

Icon Restyle lets you change how Moodle draws its icons, in one place, without touching a theme and without writing code.
You choose an icon in a grid, change its colour, its shape, add a gradient or an outline, or replace it with a file of your
own, and every page of the site shows it. A change can apply to the whole site or only to a category, a course or an
activity, to some kinds of page or some parts of a page, and to one colour mode of Moodle.

## Who it is for

- **Site managers** who want the icons of their Moodle to follow the identity of their institution.
- **Course designers** who want the icons of one course or one activity to stand out.
- **Administrators** who move the same look between a test site and production.

You need the capability `local/iconrestyle:manage` to use the designer, which managers have by default. Exporting and
importing needs `local/iconrestyle:exchange`.
